Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: bringing traditional, long-fermentation sourdough and warm hospitality back to our local neighborhood street corners. What started as a small farmer's market booth has grown into a bustling storefront loved by commuters, families, and bread enthusiasts alike.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts with honest ingredients. We source 100% of our grain flours from local stone-milling farms, use organic dairy from regional pastures, and never use artificial preservatives, additives, or dough conditioners.
